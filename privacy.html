Privacy Policy for Mob Rush

Last updated: April 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Mob Rush game ("the app") handles your information.

Information We Collect

Mob Rush does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. The game runs entirely on your device.

Local Storage

The app may store game progress (such as best score) on your device using local storage. This data is never sent anywhere and stays on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
